Interpretation & Usage Notes (v1 units: imperial / ft-lbs torque):
  • Regional 'stress' values are accumulated peak *torque demand* proxies (ft-lb × weighting).
    (Converted from internal Nm using standard factor; compare relatively within athlete.)
    Higher values mean the model estimates greater mechanical demand on that region
    across the included analyses/positions. Compare relative to the same athlete's
    other sessions or programs — absolute numbers are best treated as directional.
  • Multi-position data (bottom/mid/top) contributes more granular load than single-position
    snapshots. A week heavy on 3-position squat work will naturally show different
    regional distribution than a week using only mid-thigh pin pulls.
  • Geometric moment-arm data (when UserAnthropometry was supplied) is used where
    available. Check individual AnalysisResult notes or MultiPositionResult summaries
    for 'geometric' vs 'reference table' provenance on specific lifts.
  • Top stressed region is useful for programming emphasis decisions (e.g., 'this
    low-bar week loaded glutes ~X% more than the high-bar week for this lifter').
    Use individual result.peak_torque_ftlb for exact ft-lbs torque at joint.
  • Measurements default to inches/ft/lbs in v1; forces/torques reported in ft-lbs.
  • These numbers intentionally ignore recovery, sleep, nutrition, joint tolerance,
    and technique. They are one mechanical data layer to combine with real-world
    athlete feedback, not a complete training prescription system.
